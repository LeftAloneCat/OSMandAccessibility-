# OSMandAccessibility-
Accessibility plugins for the OSMand~ maps application

## Visual cues instead of audio directions while navigating
Audibly impaired people can have troubles not missing turns, because they cannot hear the audio cues reminding them of the turn coming up. Our idea takes something that audibly impaired people are already familiar with and actively use in their community - flashing lights as an alarm, even their schools use flashing lights instead of bells. So we implemented this idea into this plugin that flashes the screen edges of your phone/navigation, visually reminding you about a turn that is coming up.

* single flash for 500m
* double flash for 200m
* triple flash for 100m
* quadruple flash for 50m

## "Waze -like" reporting of accessible places, locations and businesses 

A plugin adding review/reporting functionality to the OSMand application. All businesses and places are already marked, this plugin would add a section describing the accessibility of said place. The plugin incorporates publicly available data online and incorporates it into one place, as well as giving people the ability to report the accessibility levels / what kind of services does the business provide for people with disabilities.
