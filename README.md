# OSMandAccessibility-
Accessibility plugins for the OSMand~ maps application

### What is OSM?
OpenStreetMap a mapping database built by a community of mappers that contribute and maintain data about roads, trails, cafés, railway stations, and much more, all over the world. 

### What is OSMand~?
OsmAnd (OSM Automated Navigation Directions) is a map and navigation application with access to the free, worldwide, and high-quality OpenStreetMap (OSM) database. All map data can be stored on your device's memory card for offline use. Via your device's GPS, OsmAnd offers routing, with optical and voice guidance, for car, bike, and pedestrian usage. All main functionalities work both online and offline (no internet needed).

## Visual cues instead of audio directions while navigating
Audibly impaired people can have troubles not missing turns, because they cannot hear the audio cues reminding them of the turn coming up. Our idea takes something that audibly impaired people are already familiar with and actively use in their community - flashing lights as an alarm, even their schools use flashing lights instead of bells. So we implemented this idea into this plugin that flashes the screen edges of your phone/navigation, visually reminding you about a turn that is coming up.

* single flash for 500m
* double flash for 200m
* triple flash for 100m
* quadruple flash for 50m

## "Waze -like" reporting of accessible places, locations and businesses 
A plugin adding review/reporting functionality to the OSMand application. All businesses and places are already marked, this plugin would add a section describing the accessibility of said place. The plugin incorporates publicly available data online and incorporates it into one place, as well as giving people the ability to report the accessibility levels / what kind of services does the business provide for people with disabilities. 

## Phone audibly announcing what bus has arrived for the visually impaired
Not possible in OSMand, since it has bus routes and schedules, but not live locations of busses. So the announcing would be quite unreliable, because Vilnius's busses are often off schedule ;). However such functionality can be extremely useful if inplemented in apps such as Traffi and JUDU, which feature live bus locations.

## AI Functionality for the blind
A navigating profile that only picks the roads that have built in navigating texture tiles for visually impaired, allowing people with such disabilities go places by themselves without fear.

(Not in OSMand) An application that has full access of the camera, allowing blind people to walk around pointing the camera forwards, audibly announcing dangers like a sudden end of the road, "describe what is this item" functionality as a big button.
